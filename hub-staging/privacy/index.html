Privacy

No cookies, no accounts, no ads, nothing sold.

Fourth Meridian Games is run by James Larsen. The games are static pages: there is no sign-in and no server that stores anything about you, except where a game says so below.

Counting visits

The site and the games count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device; Cloudflare reports to me only totals such as page views, countries and referring sites. It runs only on fourthmeridiangames.com, never on test copies.

Trendle

Trendle has its own privacy page: Trendle privacy.

Streetle

Streetle sets no cookies and has no accounts. It keeps your results and today's progress, in your browser's local storage on your device; that never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

It loads the map data, fonts and code from this site only. On fourthmeridiangames.com it also counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (above) and loads the "More from Fourth Meridian" list of other games from this site.

Diplomacy School

Diplomacy School sets no cookies and has no accounts. It keeps which lessons you have finished, in your browser's local storage on your device; that never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

It loads its code and map from this site, and its fonts from Google Fonts (so Google sees your IP address when the fonts load). On fourthmeridiangames.com it also counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (above) and loads the "More from Fourth Meridian" list of other games from this site.

Hatama

Hatama sets no cookies and has no accounts. It keeps your results, in your browser's local storage on your device; that never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

It loads its code from this site, and its Batak script font from Google Fonts (so Google sees your IP address when the font loads). On fourthmeridiangames.com it also counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics (above) and loads the "More from Fourth Meridian" list of other games from this site.
