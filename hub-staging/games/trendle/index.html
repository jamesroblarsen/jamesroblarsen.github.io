How to play

  1. Each chart shows one real data point from a public statistic, such as a country's coal share or its life expectancy.
  2. Draw how you think the statistic moved in the years around it, one point per year.
  3. Submit and the real line is drawn over yours. Every point is scored by how close you came.
  4. Stay close several points in a row to build a streak that multiplies your points.
  5. Five charts on one theme each day, the same for everyone. Share your score when you're done.

Questions

Is Trendle free?
Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up and no ads.
Where does the data come from?
From statistics agencies and other public sources. Each chart names and links its source when it is revealed, and the Credits page lists every provider and licence.
How is a chart scored?
Each point earns accuracy by how near it lands to the real value, measured as a share of the chart's height; a point a quarter of the chart away earns nothing. Points are 1000 times your average accuracy, multiplied while you keep a streak of close points.
Can I play earlier days?
No. Trendle is a daily game: five new charts each day, the same for everyone.
