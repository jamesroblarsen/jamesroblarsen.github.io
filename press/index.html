Trendle

Draw the rest of history.

In a sentence: See one real data point from a public statistic, draw how it changed over the years around it, then watch the real line appear and get scored. Five charts on one theme daily.

Daily chart-drawing game. Each chart shows one real data point from a public statistic (statistics agencies and Our World in Data). Draw how you think it changed over the surrounding years, then the real line is revealed and your line is scored for accuracy. Five questions on one theme each day, the same for everyone. Free, no login.

Play: https://fourthmeridiangames.com/trendle/ · How to play · Share image (1200×630 PNG)

Streetle

Name the city from its streets.

In a sentence: Name the city from an unlabelled 3D model of its street plan and buildings. Six guesses, and each wrong one reveals a clue about the city.

Daily city-guessing game. Each day shows an unlabelled 3D model of a small piece of a real city, chosen to show how it was planned or grew. Name the city in six guesses. Each wrong guess widens the view and adds a clue: real building heights, then kinds of place picked for that city (such as places of worship or where people drink) and a few street names, then the country. One city a day, the same for everyone. Free, no login.

Play: https://fourthmeridiangames.com/streetle/ · How to play · Share image (1200×630 PNG)
