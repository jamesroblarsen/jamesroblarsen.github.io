Glossle

Five-letter word puzzles in the world's other languages.

From Greek glōssa, “tongue, language”.

Five letters, a few guesses, and a language you may never have played a word game in. Each Glossle game is built from its language's own dictionaries and texts, keeps that language's alphabet, and tells you what the word means when the round is over. Free in your browser, no sign-up, no ads.

How a Glossle works

  1. Guess the hidden five-letter word. Each game says how many tries you get.
  2. After each guess the tiles show which letters are in the word and which are in the right place.
  3. The keyboard follows the language: letters it never uses are left off, and letters found only in borrowed words are faded.
  4. When the round ends you see the word and what it means.

Where the words come from

Every answer appears in at least two independent dictionaries of the language. Speakers of the language review the answer list, and any word they flag as wrong, rude or out of use comes out.

Spotted a word that shouldn't be there? Email contact@fourthmeridiangames.com with the game and the word.

Suggest a language

Glossle is for languages with living speakers but few word games of their own. If you speak one and would like to see it here, or could help check a word list, write to contact@fourthmeridiangames.com.
