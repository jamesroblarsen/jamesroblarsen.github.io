Streetle

Name the city from its streets.

  • Daily
  • Geography
  • Cities

Each day, an unlabelled 3D model of part of a real city. Read its street plan and buildings, then the clues each wrong guess reveals, and name it in six guesses. A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Free in your browser, no sign-up, no ads.

Play Streetle→

How to play

  1. Each day shows an unlabelled 3D model of a small piece of a real city, chosen to show how it was planned or how it grew.
  2. Type a city and pick it from the list. Typing a country lists its cities, biggest first.
  3. Each wrong guess widens the view and adds a clue: real building heights, then kinds of place picked for that city (such as places of worship or where people drink), then a few street names.
  4. Early guesses get yes-or-no checks such as hemisphere, continent and which side of the road people drive on. Later guesses show the distance and direction to the answer.
  5. You have six guesses. The last clue names the country and limits your final guess to it.

Questions

Is Streetle free?
Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up and no ads.
Where do the maps come from?
Streets, buildings and places come from OpenStreetMap contributors via OpenMapTiles, with extra building footprints from Overture Maps for some cities. The city list comes from Natural Earth.
When does a new city appear?
At midnight where you are. Everyone gets the same city on the same date.
Which cities can come up?
About three hundred cities on every continent, picked for street plans that show how they were planned or grew: grids, old towns, colonial plans, ring roads and cities shaped by water.
