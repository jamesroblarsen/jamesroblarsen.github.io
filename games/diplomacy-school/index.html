Diplomacy School

Learn the board game Diplomacy.

  • Learn
  • Strategy

An interactive school for Diplomacy: play through a guided campaign as Turkey to learn the rules, then the tactics, then the art of the credible lie. Work through the lessons at your own pace. Free in your browser, no sign-up, no ads.

Play Diplomacy School→

How to play

  1. Pick a track: Basic Rules, Basic Tactics or Basic Diplomacy.
  2. Basic Rules is a guided campaign as Turkey from Spring 1901, covering every rule of movement and combat, including fleets, coasts and convoys.
  3. Give orders the way online Diplomacy sites do: click a unit, then click where it should go or whom it should support.
  4. Tactics and Diplomacy lessons ask you to judge real board positions, from openings to credible and incredible lies.
  5. Your finished lessons are remembered in this browser.

Questions

Is Diplomacy School free?
Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up and no ads.
Do I need to know Diplomacy already?
No. Basic Rules starts from the first turn and builds up to the rare rules, one move at a time.
Is it official?
No. It is an unofficial tutor. Diplomacy is a trademark of its owners, and this project is not affiliated with them.
How are the moves judged?
By its own rules engine, checked against the standard Diplomacy test cases.
