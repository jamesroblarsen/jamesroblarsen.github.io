Hatama

Guess the Batak Toba word.

Guess the five-letter Batak Toba word in five tries, then see what it means. Endless: every round is a new word. Play as many rounds as you like. Free in your browser, no sign-up, no ads.

Play Hatama→

How to play

  1. Guess the hidden five-letter Batak Toba word in five tries.
  2. After each guess the tiles show which letters are in the word and which are in the right place.
  3. Faded keys are letters used only in loanwords.
  4. After each round you see the word in Batak script and what it means. Share your result, or start a new word whenever you like.

Questions

Is Hatama free?
Yes. It runs in your browser with no sign-up and no ads.
Where do the words come from?
From Batak Toba dictionaries and texts: every answer appears in two independent dictionaries.
Who checks the words?
Siska Marsinta Siahaan, a Batak Toba speaker, read every answer word. Words she marked wrong never come up as answers, though you can still type them as guesses.
Is there a new word every day?
No. Hatama is endless: every round is a new word, so you can play as many as you like.
