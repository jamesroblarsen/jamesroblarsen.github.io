Privacy

No cookies, no accounts, no ads, nothing sold.

Fourth Meridian Games is run by James Larsen. The games are static pages: there is no sign-in and no server that stores anything about you, except where a game says so below.

Counting visits

I count visits to see how many people play and where they find the games. The counters below keep totals only: no cookies, no IDs, nothing that tells me who you are, and nothing shared with anyone else. They run only on fourthmeridiangames.com, never on test copies.

Cloudflare Web Analytics counts page views. It stores nothing on your device and reports totals such as page views, countries and referring sites.

Return visits. To count how many players come back, your browser keeps one small note in its local storage, named fm:visits: for each game, the first and last day you visited and on how many days. Once a day per game it sends a report to my own counter at pulse.fourthmeridiangames.com saying only: new or returning, roughly how long since your last visit, roughly how many days you have visited, and which site linked you here. The counter adds 1 to that day's totals (with your country, worked out from the connection and not stored) and keeps nothing else: no IP address, no record of you. The note on your device resets after 13 months, and the totals are deleted after 25 months.

Finished games. Games that say so below also count finished daily puzzles. When you finish one, your browser sends my counter the game, the puzzle number, whether you solved it and in how many guesses. The counter adds 1 to that day's totals and keeps nothing else, and these totals are also deleted after 25 months. When you finish, the game shows you everyone's totals for that puzzle: how many played and how many solved it in each number of guesses.

To opt out, turn on Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track in your browser: then no report is sent, no finished game is counted, and no note is kept. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes the note.

Streetle

Streetle sets no cookies and has no accounts. It keeps your results and today's progress, in your browser's local storage on your device; that never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

It loads the map data, fonts and code from this site, and after you finish, the day's totals from the counter at pulse.fourthmeridiangames.com. On fourthmeridiangames.com it also counts visits as described above and loads the "More from Fourth Meridian" list of other games from this site.

When you finish the day's map, it sends a finished-game count as described above: the puzzle number, whether you solved it, and in how many guesses. It then shows you everyone's totals for that map.

Diplomacy School

Diplomacy School sets no cookies and has no accounts. It keeps which lessons you have finished, in your browser's local storage on your device; that never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

It loads its code and map from this site, and its fonts from Google Fonts (so Google sees your IP address when the fonts load). On fourthmeridiangames.com it also counts visits as described above and loads the "More from Fourth Meridian" list of other games from this site.

Hatama

Hatama sets no cookies and has no accounts. It keeps your results, in your browser's local storage on your device; that never leaves your device, and clearing your browser's site data removes it.

It loads its code from this site, and its Batak script font from Google Fonts (so Google sees your IP address when the font loads). On fourthmeridiangames.com it also counts visits as described above and loads the "More from Fourth Meridian" list of other games from this site.

When you finish the day's word, it sends a finished-game count as described above: the puzzle number, whether you solved it, and in how many guesses. It then shows you everyone's totals for that word.

Questions

Email contact@fourthmeridiangames.com, or use the form on the contact page. Email you send reaches me through Cloudflare Email Routing and is kept in my mailbox. The form sends your name, email address and message through my server at pulse.fourthmeridiangames.com to that same mailbox; the server keeps no copy, only a count of messages each day.
